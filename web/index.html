Split a private key into SLIP-39 mnemonic shares — recoverable from a threshold of groups, each needing a threshold of members. Same key, same curve as the Convert tab: paste any hex/WIF/mnemonic form of it.

SLIP-39 always encrypts, even with an empty passphrase. Whatever you set here, write it down with the shares — it is not verified when recovering (see Combine).

Advanced

PBKDF2 cost is 2500 × 2^exponent. Higher is slower to brute-force but slower to recover too. 0 is fine for most uses.

Recombine enough shares (meeting the group and member thresholds they were split with) back into the original private key.
